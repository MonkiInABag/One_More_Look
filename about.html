About One More Look

Preloved fashion with care behind every piece.

One More Look is a small Cheshire clothing business built around careful resale: hand-picked pieces, honest descriptions, and regular drops that make preloved clothing feel exciting again.

Curated

We choose pieces with shape, quality, and wear left in them. The aim is a collection that feels edited, not overwhelming.

Honest

Every listing should tell the truth: size, condition, fabric feel, and anything a buyer would want to know before ordering.

Local

Built from Cheshire, One More Look is made for customers who want beautiful clothes with less waste and more character.

Curated preloved clothing from Cheshire, honestly described and ready for another wear.
