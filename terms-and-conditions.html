Terms and Conditions

Our terms for buying and selling with One More Look.

These terms outline how orders, listings, returns, and seller submissions are handled on this website.

Orders

Product listings are offered subject to availability. If an item becomes unavailable after an order request is placed, we will contact you as soon as possible.

Preloved condition

Items are preloved unless clearly stated otherwise. We aim to describe condition, sizing, and visible wear honestly before purchase.

Payment and delivery

Payment and delivery details are confirmed during checkout or direct communication. Standard delivery takes 3-5 working days on average. Next day delivery is also available, with an order cut-off time of 1pm.

Returns

Please contact us promptly if there is a problem with your order. Return eligibility depends on the issue, item condition, and any agreed sale terms.

Selling to us

Seller submissions are reviewed before acceptance. We may decline items that do not match our current buying needs, condition standards, or resale style.

Contact

For questions about these terms, orders, or selling clothes to One More Look, please use the contact page and we will get back to you.
